Black Hole Detective黑洞侦探

A black hole that isn't swallowing anything gives off no light at all. But its gravity still pulls on any star orbiting it, so that star traces a tiny loop on the sky. The Gaia space telescope measures those loops. This page shows, step by step, how astronomers use them to find black holes nobody can see.一个没有在吞噬物质的黑洞完全不发光。但它的引力仍然拉着绕它运行的恒星，让这颗恒星在天空中画出一个微小的圈。盖亚（Gaia）空间望远镜能测量这些小圈。这个页面一步步展示天文学家怎样利用它们，找到谁也看不见的黑洞。

The method方法

How the detective work goes侦探工作的五个步骤

  1. Watch the star wobble观察恒星的摆动

    From 2014 to 2017, Gaia measured the positions of about 1.8 billion stars again and again. For 169,208 binaries with well-measured distances, it fitted an orbit to the wobble.2014 至 2017 年间，Gaia 反复测量了约 18 亿颗恒星的位置。对其中 169,208 个距离测得较准的双星，它为摆动拟合出了轨道。

  2. Turn the loop into a mass把圈换算成质量

    Loop size, distance and period go into Kepler's law. Out comes the mass function: the least the hidden partner can weigh.把圈的大小、距离和周期代入开普勒定律，就得到质量函数：隐藏伴星最少有多重。

  3. Ask: is the partner dark?问：伴星是暗的吗？

    A normal star over 3 Suns would shine brightly, and neutron stars can't weigh more than about 3 Suns. So a dark partner above 3 Suns must be a black hole.超过 3 倍太阳质量的普通恒星会非常亮，而中子星最多约 3 倍太阳质量。所以，一个超过 3 倍太阳质量又不发光的伴星，一定是黑洞。

  4. Rule out glitches排除测量假象

    Most candidates turn out to be measurement problems. Astronomers check each one for warning signs.大多数候选者其实是测量出了问题。天文学家会逐一检查它们的警示信号。

  5. Confirm with another telescope用其他望远镜确认

    Ground telescopes measure the star's speed from tiny colour shifts in its light (the Doppler effect). Gaia BH1 and BH2 were confirmed this way in 2022–2023.地面望远镜通过恒星光线颜色的微小变化（多普勒效应）测出它的速度。Gaia BH1 和 BH2 就是在 2022–2023 年这样被确认的。
